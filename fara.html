THE MISSION

FARA // fará — "village" in the Rohingya language
Every village had a name. FARA exists so those names are never erased.

Across Arakan (Rakhine State, Myanmar), the names of Rohingya villages — spoken for generations — are being systematically renamed, depopulated, and wiped from official maps. When a name disappears, the memory of the people who lived there begins to disappear with it.

FARA is a digital archive of 1,023 villages across Maungdaw, Buthidaung, Rathedaung and Sittwe townships. Each record preserves the village's Rohingya name, its official romanized name, its Burmese-script name, its village tract, and its exact coordinates.

The archive is built to support research, reporting, documentation, and evidence-based understanding of Rohingya village names and locations — for journalists, researchers, human-rights documentation, and the Rohingya community itself.

Source data: Rohingya Khobor — Arakan Maps Project. Records with known data issues are flagged, never silently altered.

A project by AB Aziz · Contact — ab@rohingya.ro
