Maungdaw township · Arakan

A village called Fuwa Khali, Bor Para

Fuwa Khali, Bor Para is a Rohingya village in Hpar Wut Chaung (Ywar Thit) village tract, Maungdaw township, in the north of Arakan, the land Myanmar calls Rakhine State.

On official maps it is written Hpar Wat Chaung (Ywar Haung).

A century ago

On the map of 1890–1906

British surveyors mapped this ground long before any satellite passed over it. The ring marks where Fuwa Khali, Bor Para is recorded today.

The spellings on the sheet are the surveyors’ own, and a position can be off by a hundred metres or more.

Between the two pictures

1day on which a satellite
detected fire here

NASA’s fire-detecting satellites pass over several times a day. They recorded fire within one kilometre of Fuwa Khali, Bor Para on 22 September 2017. The nearest detection was 41 metres from the village’s recorded position.

A detection is heat seen from orbit. It does not say what burned, and fires under cloud are not seen at all. Days are in Myanmar time.

After

28 April 2018

The same ground, in the earliest picture the archive holds from after that autumn. The yellow line is what UNOSAT, the United Nations satellite centre, mapped as destroyed: about 6.57 hectares within 300 metres of the village’s recorded position, first seen in a satellite image of 25 September 2017.

UNOSAT worked from satellite images alone and did not check its findings on the ground.

Not one village alone

Fuwa Khali, Bor Para is one of 209 villages in Maungdaw township where UNOSAT mapped destroyed buildings at the recorded position. Another 53 had a destroyed zone within 500 metres.

The survey used pictures taken between 31 August and 11 October 2017. It cannot show what happened after that date.

Sources and method

  • UNOSAT: Destroyed areas in Buthidaung, Maungdaw, and Rathedaung Townships of Rakhine State in Myanmar — the destroyed zones and their dates. CC BY-SA. Analysis from satellite imagery, not validated on the ground.
  • NASA FIRMS: active fire detections (VIIRS 375 m and MODIS 1 km) — the fires and their days, in Myanmar time. A detection is heat seen from orbit; fires under cloud are not detected.
  • Esri World Imagery Wayback — the satellite pictures and their capture dates.
  • Rohingya Khobor: Arakan Maps Project — the village name and position.
  • UN OCHA: Myanmar Subnational Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB, source: MIMU) — township boundaries. CC BY-IGO.
  • Old Survey of India Maps: georeferenced maps of Burma — the survey map, CC BY 4.0

How villages were matched: a village is counted as destroyed when a UNOSAT zone covers its recorded position or at least one hectare is marked destroyed within 300 metres of it, and as “nearby” when any zone lies within 500 metres. Matching is by position only and has not been reviewed village by village. The pictures are shown as Esri published them; FARA does not describe what they show. The per-village results are in status_unosat_2017.csv.
