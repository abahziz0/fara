# Licence of the data

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code of this repository (the site and the build tools). The data is licensed separately.

## FARA's own part

The compilation, the corrections and the matching made by FARA are published under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may copy, share and adapt it for any purpose, as long as you credit FARA. Please cite the edition you used:
see `CITATION.cff` or https://map.rohingya.ro/methods/#editions-and-how-to-cite
