# Further sources: old maps, United Nations reports and other records

FARA is built on a small number of sources, listed on the [methods page](/methods/). This page lists other records about the same villages: old maps, United Nations reports, satellite studies and name lists. Some are now shown on the map or on village records. The rest are here so that a reader can go to them directly.

## Old maps

The old-map layer on the map is made from survey maps drawn under British rule. Each sheet was scanned and placed on modern coordinates by the Old Survey of India Maps community and by Horst Held, who share them under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. FARA then checked every sheet and corrected ten of them against their own printed grid; the [methods page](/methods/#old-maps-united-nations-reports-and-other-spellings) says how, and how close each sheet now sits.

| Layer | Sheets | Surveyed or printed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Around 1900 | One inch to a mile, sheets 84 C/3-7, 84 C/4-8, 84 D/1-5, 84 D/2-6 and 84 D/11-15 | 1890 to 1906 | [Georeferenced historic 63k maps of Burma](https://zenodo.org/records/18975982) |
| 1925 to 1944 | One inch to a mile, sheets 84 D/9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15 and 16 | 1925 to 1936 | [Georeferenced 63k maps of Burma](https://zenodo.org/records/11367062) |
| 1925 to 1944 | Half inch to a mile, sheets 84 C/SW, 84 C/SE, 84 D/NW and 84 D/SE, where no one-inch sheet is available | 1926 to 1942 | [Georeferenced half-inch maps of Burma](https://zenodo.org/records/13346102) |

On the map, choose a period under Old maps. "Lines only" hides the paper and lays the drawn lines and names over today's satellite picture. In the picture viewer of a village, "Old map" puts the survey map beside the newest picture. The video of a village opens with the survey maps of its ground.

What to keep in mind: the spellings on these maps are the surveyors' own and are often far from today's names. A village missing from an old sheet was not necessarily absent. A one-inch map was drawn to about 30 metres at best, and rivers and coasts have moved since.

## United Nations reports

- **[The Destruction and Dispossession of Rohingya Land and Property during the 2017 Clearance Operations: Public Summary](https://iimm.un.org/sites/default/files/2025/09/Land%20Report%20Public%20Summary%20EN.pdf)**, Independent Investigative Mechanism for Myanmar, 29 September 2025. It follows seven village tracts where Rohingya villages were destroyed and Border Guard Police bases were then built. Where it names a village in this archive, the village record carries a short line from it.
- **[Report of the detailed findings of the Independent International Fact-Finding Mission on Myanmar](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/1643079)**, A/HRC/39/CRP.2, 17 September 2018. It gives dated accounts for named villages and a satellite count of about 392 villages destroyed in part or whole.
- **[Detailed findings of the Fact-Finding Mission, 2019](https://www.ohchr.org/Documents/HRBodies/HRCouncil/FFM-Myanmar/20190916/A_HRC_42_CRP.5.pdf)**, A/HRC/42/CRP.5, 16 September 2019.
- **[The economic interests of the Myanmar military](https://www.ohchr.org/en/hr-bodies/hrc/myanmar-ffm/economic-interests-myanmar-military)**, A/HRC/42/CRP.3, 2019.
- **[UNOSAT analysis of destruction and other developments in Rakhine State](https://www.ohchr.org/Documents/HRBodies/HRCouncil/FFM-Myanmar/UNOSATReportMyanmar_20180912.pdf)**, 2018. The written method behind the satellite results this archive uses.

## Satellite studies by others

- **[The Ocelli Project: Rakhine](https://ocelli.c4ads.org/)**, C4ADS, 2021. A village-by-village satellite record of destruction.
- **[Mapping conditions in Rakhine State](https://pageflow.aspi.org.au/rakhine-state)**, Australian Strategic Policy Institute, 2019. What happened to the destroyed settlements afterwards.
- **[Remaking Rakhine State](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/asa16/8018/2018/en/)**, Amnesty International, 2018. Bases and roads built on burned villages.
- **Human Rights Watch**: [Scores of Rohingya villages bulldozed](https://www.hrw.org/news/2018/02/23/burma-scores-rohingya-villages-bulldozed), 2018; [the Arakan Army massacre at Hoyyar Siri](https://www.hrw.org/report/2026/05/18/skeletons-and-skulls-scattered-everywhere/arakan-army-massacre-of-rohingya), on Buthidaung in 2024.
- **[Violence and mortality in the Northern Rakhine State of Myanmar, 2017](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanplh/article/PIIS2542-5196(19)30037-3/fulltext)**, Physicians for Human Rights, in The Lancet Planetary Health, 2019. A survey of the leaders of about 600 Rohingya hamlets.

## Names and history

- **[GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/)**, CC BY 4.0. The source of the "other spellings" on village records: mostly the older romanisations used on twentieth-century maps.
- **Today's roads, rivers and coast**: © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors. Used only to check that the old maps are placed correctly.
- **[Burma Gazetteer: Akyab District, volume A](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.210387)**, R. B. Smart, 1917. The colonial district handbook, free to read at the Internet Archive.
- **[British Library, India Office Records, IOR/V/27/64](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/037-000145152)**. The catalogue of the Burma gazetteers, with the Akyab statistical tables of 1906, 1912 and 1924.
- **[MIMU township profiles](https://themimu.info/township-profiles)**. Government township reports of 2019, in Burmese.

## A note on care

Several of these reports describe killings and other violence in named villages. This archive records places and names. Where a report is cited on a village record, the line is short and points to the report, so that the account is read in full and in its own words.
