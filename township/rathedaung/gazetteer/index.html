About this gazetteerThe villages of Rathedaung, by name

This booklet lists the 214 Rohingya villages of Rathedaung township that FARA has on record: each one's name in Rohingya, its official name, its name in Burmese script, where it is, and its official place code. It is made to be printed, kept and carried.

Why it exists

Rohingya village names are being renamed, left off official maps and forgotten as villages empty. FARA (“fará” is the Rohingya word for village) keeps them on record. A gazetteer on paper does not depend on a website staying online.

How it is arranged

Villages are grouped by village tract, and the tracts run in alphabetical order, each with a number. Within a tract the villages run A to Z by Rohingya name. Every village has an entry number, used on the small map of its tract and in the index at the back, which lists both Rohingya and official names.

Position20.55077, 92.65051

Positions are latitude and longitude in decimal degrees (WGS84). The code is the MIMU place code (“P-code”) that the United Nations and aid agencies use for the village; a dash means it has none.

Where a name has not been recorded yet, the gazetteer says so rather than guessing. In this township 6 of 214 villages have a recorded Rohingya name, 214 a Burmese-script name and 214 an official place code.

The 2017 column

The last column shows what UNOSAT, the United Nations satellite centre, marked at or near each village in satellite imagery taken between 31 August and 11 October 2017.

  • Destroyed. Destroyed buildings were mapped at the village. 15 villages in this township.
  • Destruction nearby. A destroyed zone lies within 500 metres; it may belong to a neighbouring settlement. 3 villages.
  • None detected. No destroyed zone within 500 metres. 196 villages.
  • –Not assessed. Outside the area UNOSAT analysed, or the location is unverified. 0 villages.

“None detected” does not mean a village is standing. The imagery ends on 11 October 2017, parts of the area were under cloud, destruction continued after that date, and UNOSAT did not validate its analysis on the ground.

Corrections

If a name is missing, misspelt or in the wrong place, please say so: ab@rohingya.ro, or the “Suggest a name or correction” button on any village at map.rohingya.ro. Records with known problems are flagged, never silently changed.

AboutFARA, the Arakan Village Name Archive

FARA preserves the names and locations of 1,023 Rohingya villages across Maungdaw, Buthidaung, Rathedaung and Sittwe townships of Arakan, in Rohingya, English and Burmese. Every village had a name. FARA exists so those names are never erased.

Online

The full archive, with satellite imagery of every village, is at map.rohingya.ro. This township is at map.rohingya.ro/township/rathedaung/, and the newest edition of this gazetteer at map.rohingya.ro/downloads/fara-gazetteer-rathedaung.pdf.

Using this gazetteer

You are free to print, copy and share it. The village names and positions come from the Rohingya Khobor Arakan Maps Project. Place codes are © Myanmar Information Management Unit. The boundaries are from UN OCHA's COD-AB dataset (source: MIMU; CC BY-IGO) and the 2017 analysis from UNOSAT (CC BY-SA); please keep those credits with the maps and the 2017 column.
